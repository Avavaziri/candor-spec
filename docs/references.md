# References

Candor cites only sources that the editors have read.
The editors checked every link on 25 November 2026.
Quotes in the patterns are short and come from these pages.

## Normative references

These documents define words or rules that Candor depends on.

- **BCP 14.** S. Bradner, [RFC 2119: Key words for use in RFCs to Indicate Requirement Levels](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119.html), March 1997. B. Leiba, [RFC 8174: Ambiguity of Uppercase vs Lowercase in RFC 2119 Key Words](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174.html), May 2017.
- **WCAG 2.2.** W3C, [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), W3C Recommendation, first published 5 October 2023, updated 12 December 2024.

## Interaction guidance

- **Microsoft HAX.** Amershi et al., "Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction", CHI 2019, [doi:10.1145/3290605.3300233](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233). The guidelines are also in the [HAX Toolkit library](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/library/). Candor uses guidelines G1, G2, G9, G10, G11, G16 and G17.
- **Google PAIR.** People + AI Research, [People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook/). Chapters: [Explainability + Trust](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/), [Errors + Graceful Failure](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/errors-failing/), [Feedback + Control](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/feedback-controls/), [Data Collection + Evaluation](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/data-collection/), [Mental Models](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/mental-models/).
- **Apple HIG.** Apple, Human Interface Guidelines: [Machine learning](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/machine-learning) and [Generative AI](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/generative-ai).
- **WAI-ARIA APG.** W3C WAI, ARIA Authoring Practices Guide: [Dialog (Modal)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/), [Disclosure (Show/Hide)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/) and [Tooltip](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tooltip/). The Tooltip pattern is still marked as work in progress.
- **The Shape of AI.** Emily Campbell, [The Shape of AI](https://www.shapeof.ai/), a library of UX patterns for AI. Candor differs because it states testable requirements.
- **ASD-STE100.** ASD, [Simplified Technical English](https://www.asd-ste100.org/), Issue 9, January 2025. Candor follows its rules for requirements and checklists.

## Risk and security frameworks

- **OWASP.** OWASP Gen AI Security Project, [Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/). Candor uses [LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/), [LLM02:2025 Sensitive Information Disclosure](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm022025-sensitive-information-disclosure/), [LLM05:2025 Improper Output Handling](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm052025-improper-output-handling/), [LLM06:2025 Excessive Agency](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/), [LLM07:2025 System Prompt Leakage](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm072025-system-prompt-leakage/) and [LLM09:2025 Misinformation](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm092025-misinformation/).
- **NIST AI RMF.** NIST, [Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1), NIST AI 100-1, January 2023.
- **NIST Generative AI Profile.** NIST, [AI RMF: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1), NIST AI 600-1, July 2024. Candor uses risks 2 Confabulation, 4 Data Privacy, 8 Information Integrity and 9 Information Security.
- **Lethal trifecta.** Simon Willison, ["The lethal trifecta for AI agents: private data, untrusted content, and external communication"](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/16/the-lethal-trifecta/), 16 June 2025.

## Law and provenance

Candor is not legal advice. These texts explain why some patterns exist.

- **GDPR.** [Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). Article 5(1)(c) data minimisation, Article 5(1)(e) storage limitation, Article 7 conditions for consent, Article 25 data protection by design and by default.
- **EU AI Act.** [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng), Article 50, "Transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems". The editors checked the original text only, not later amendments.
- **C2PA.** Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity, [Content Credentials: C2PA Technical Specification](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.5/specs/C2PA_Specification.html), version 2.5, October 2026.
